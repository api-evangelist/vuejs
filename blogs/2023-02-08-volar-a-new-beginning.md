Most users of Volar know it as the official Vue.js VSCode extension. It started as a personal project when the official recommendation was still Vetur, and over time got adopted as the new official extension due to improved architecture and performance. As a project created to improve the quality of life for developers, we spent over two years before reaching 1.0 , and have been continuously shipping stability improvements.
